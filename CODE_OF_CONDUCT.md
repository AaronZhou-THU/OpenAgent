# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project expects contributors and maintainers to act professionally and respectfully.

- Be respectful in disagreements.
- Critique ideas, code, and decisions, not people.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks.
- Assume good faith, but be precise when pointing out technical problems.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the project for users and contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment or hateful conduct
- Threats, intimidation, or stalking
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion after being asked to stop
- Bad-faith reporting, trolling, or abuse of review processes

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainers through a private channel when possible. For security-sensitive misconduct or disclosure concerns, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
